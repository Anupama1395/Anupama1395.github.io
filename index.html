Real-time detection of plant health from UAV imagery

Strawberry plants show signs of stress well before they are visible from the ground, and manual inspection of every row is slow and error-prone. My research at Cal Poly Pomona addresses this with a detection system that runs in the field.

I built a PyTorch pipeline that joins UAV imagery, EXIF-GPS coordinates, field annotations and proximal sensor readings. On this data I trained and compared YOLOv8, YOLO11, DETR and Faster R-CNN, evaluating each with mAP, per-class AP, precision, recall and confusion matrices to select a deployment model.

The selected YOLOv8 model runs on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano with live camera input, exported to ONNX and TensorRT FP16 for on-device inference. Hierarchical ResNet and EfficientNet classifiers then grade plants into ordered health categories. The results are published in the Proceedings of SPIE.

Deep Learning Models with a Hierarchical Method Using RGB Images from UAV and Proximal Sensor Data for Real-Time Detection of Strawberry Plant Health

Anupama Singh, S. Bhandari, A. Raheja, S. C. Kosaraju

Proceedings of SPIE, Vol. 14045, June 2026

We combine UAV RGB imagery with proximal sensor data to detect strawberry plant health in real time. A pipeline joins imagery, EXIF-GPS coordinates, field annotations and sensor readings, yielding more than 400 frames and 9,000 annotations. YOLOv8, YOLO11, DETR and Faster R-CNN are compared for detection, and hierarchical ResNet and EfficientNet classifiers assign ordered health categories. The deployed YOLOv8 model runs on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano with TensorRT FP16 in preliminary field tests.

Lunar Landing Site and Terrain Hazard Analysis

Anupama Singh · Researcher, Cal Poly Pomona

This work identifies terrain hazard candidates in 5 m/pixel lunar elevation models. It compares morphological detection with a U-Net trained on synthetic patches, incorporating terrain derivatives as inputs. Outputs include candidate catalogs and density maps, with validation against LROC NAC shadow measurements in preparation and image-resolution limits documented.

Synthetic Data Generation for Healthcare across 27 MIMIC-III Tables

Anupama Singh · Research Volunteer, AI & MLX Lab

Access to patient data is restricted by privacy regulations, which limits research and reproducibility. This work builds data cleaning and synthesis workflows across 27 MIMIC-III tables using CTGAN, TVAE, VAE and Gaussian copulas to create reproducible clinical ML datasets. Real and synthetic distributions are compared, and random forest and decision tree classifiers assess downstream predictive utility. In one evaluation, a random forest predicting in-hospital mortality reached 86% accuracy trained on real data and 84% trained on synthetic data.
